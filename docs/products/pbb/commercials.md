## Revenue Model

**[CONFIRMED]** Kenswitch earns a per-transaction fee, structured differently by use case:

- **Lipa na Bank (merchant payments):** 0.5% of transaction value, capped at KES 500 — proposed specifically to a partner as the Merchant Payment Processor rate. 
- **Fund My Wallet (wallet top-up):** flat KES 10 per transaction.
